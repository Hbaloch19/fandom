ABOUT US

A digital universe built for people who love fictional worlds.

WHY FANDOMVERSE?

Fans today jump between fan wikis, streaming services, social media, ticketing sites and online stores just to keep up with one fandom. FandomVerse brings seven worlds that inspire millions of fans into a single, centralized home: Anime, Gaming, Movies, TV Shows, K-Pop, Comics and Manga.

Our goal is to create one beautiful digital space where fans can discover characters, explore stories, browse merch, catch up on events and find inspiration from their favorite fictional and real-world universes — without switching tabs a dozen times.

FandomVerse is a front-end-only portal: content is loaded from local JSON files, and an on-site AI assistant (rule-based, no external service) helps you navigate. No account, no server, no clutter — just the fandoms you love.
